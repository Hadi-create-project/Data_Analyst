### 01. End-to-End E-Commerce Data Analytics
**Optimizing Sales, Promotion Efficiency & Mitigating Revenue Leakage**

- **Problem:** Understand shopping patterns, evaluate discount effectiveness, and find the cause of high order cancellations.
- **Process:** Excel cleaning (20,150 to 19,496 rows) → BigQuery datamart (19,496 × 25) → Python EDA → Looker Studio dashboard.
- **Key results:** Net revenue IDR 9.47 B · 16,639 successful orders · AOV IDR 569,099 · cancellation rate 14.65%.
- **Insights:** Millennials (25-39) drive most revenue in Electronics & Sports; discounts above 15% gave the lowest volume; cancellations are uniform (~14%) across payment methods, pointing to a systemic checkout problem.

**Tools:** Excel · BigQuery · Python · Looker Studio
**Repository:** [Ecommerce-Project](https://github.com/Hadi-create-project/Ecommerce-Project)

### 02. Customer Segmentation: RFM & K-Means
**E-Commerce Customer & Sales Analysis (500K+ transactions)**

- **Problem:** Identify which customers contribute most revenue and how ML segmentation can improve marketing.
- **Process:** Data quality check (541,909 to 397,884 valid transactions) → BigQuery EDA → RFM scoring → K-Means (K=4).
- **Key results:** 4,338 customers · peak revenue in Nov 2011 (+352% vs monthly average) · Silhouette Score 0.68.
- **Insights:** Strong Q4 seasonality; the High-Value VIP cluster is 14% of customers but 52% of revenue.

**Tools:** SQL · BigQuery · Python (Pandas, Scikit-learn)
**Repository:** [Open repo](https://github.com/Hadi-create-project/REPLACE-REPO-NAME-02)

### 03. Customer Churn Analysis (Telco)
**From raw data to measurable retention recommendations**

- **Problem:** 26.54% of customers churn, but high-risk segments and drivers were unknown.
- **Process:** BigQuery cleaning + feature engineering → layered EDA (univariate to multivariate) → Chi-square tests → XGBoost → Power BI dashboard (3 pages).
- **Key results:** 7,043 customers · 1,869 churned · XGBoost recall 79%, precision 0.55.
- **Insights:** Month-to-month contracts in the first 12 months are the red zone (51.4% churn; 70.2% for Fiber optic). Protection add-ons and auto-pay reduce churn.

**Tools:** BigQuery · Python · XGBoost · Power BI
**Repository:** [Open repo](https://github.com/Hadi-create-project/REPLACE-REPO-NAME-03)

### 04. Marketing Campaign Analysis
**Customer Segmentation & Campaign Response Prediction**

- **Problem:** Find out who responds to campaigns, which segments and channels matter, and who to target next.
- **Process:** PostgreSQL cleaning + feature engineering → Welch t-test → RFM segmentation → Logistic Regression with recall optimization → Tableau dashboard.
- **Key results:** 2,213 valid customers · response rate 15.05% · Champions respond at 31.03% (about 6× Lost Customers) · recall improved 15% → 73%.
- **Insights:** Responders have higher income, spending, and more recent purchases (all p < 0.001). Campaign 4 performed best.

**Tools:** PostgreSQL · Python · Tableau · Logistic Regression
**Repository:** [Open repo](https://github.com/Hadi-create-project/REPLACE-REPO-NAME-04)

### 05. Amazon Products Sales Analysis 2023
**Marketplace intelligence: pricing, discounts, ratings & popularity**

- **Problem:** Understand which pricing and discount patterns actually drive trust and market traction.
- **Process:** BigQuery profiling → staging and dedup (by ASIN) → fact/mart layers → Python EDA → Spearman correlation per category → rule-based segmentation (Best Seller, Hidden Gem, Overhyped, Risky) → Looker Studio.
- **Insights:** Discount vs review count correlation is significant in 7 of 10 categories, but in different directions (positive in home_kitchen, negative in electronics).

**Tools:** BigQuery · Python (SciPy) · Looker Studio
**Repository:** [Open repo](https://github.com/Hadi-create-project/REPLACE-REPO-NAME-05)
